# Our Services

Alexis offers professional massage therapy designed to help relieve pain, reduce stress, and improve overall physical function. Each treatment is customized to meet your unique needs and goals, delivered in a safe and calming environment.

Services include therapeutic massage for injury recovery, relaxation massage for nervous system regulation, and focused treatments for conditions such as chronic pain, TMJ dysfunction, stress-related tension, and sports injuries. Alexis has a special interest in working with individuals managing anxiety, persistent pain, and TMJ-related concerns.

Whether you're seeking support for a specific condition or looking to improve your general well-being, treatments are grounded in evidence-informed practice and a patient-centered approach that prioritizes comfort, trust, and lasting results.
