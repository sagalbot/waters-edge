### 60 min Pregnancy Massage Treatment

Pregnancy massage, also known as prenatal massage, is massage therapy that is designed to support the physical and emotional well-being of pregnant women/persons. It is a gentle and therapeutic form of massage that is tailored to the unique needs and changes that occur in a woman's/persons body during pregnancy. During a pregnancy massage the therapist will use a pregnancy pillow or have the client in various positions ( side lying or semi fowlers) to make the massage as comfortable and safe for the mom and baby.

## Discipline description: Registered Massage Therapy

Massage therapy is a hands-on technique that involves the manipulation of muscles and soft tissues to relieve tension, reduce stress, improve circulation, and promote overall relaxation and well-being. It can help alleviate pain, enhance mobility, and support recovery from injuries. Each treatment use various modalities and are tailored to individual needs and health goals.

Since 2015, Alexis has been a dedicated Registered Massage Therapist, blending scientific knowledge with an intuitive and compassionate approach to provide personalized, therapeutic treatments. She recognizes the deep connection between physical and mental well-being and creates a safe, supportive space for healing.

Believing that every person is unique, Alexis tailors each treatment to individual needs, emphasizing patient education and self-awareness as key components of recovery. She remains committed to ongoing learning, with a focus on holistic approaches that integrate evidence-based techniques.

Her passion for healing is deeply personal, shaped by her own experiences recovering from injuries, including a serious car accident and the physical demands of an active lifestyle. Outside the clinic, Alexis embraces the outdoors, often hiking, trail running, or mountain biking. North Vancouver’s natural beauty and active lifestyle inspire her daily, and she is grateful to call it home.
