---
title: TMJ Dysfunction
order: 5
summary: "Treatment focuses on the jaw and neck muscles, including intra-oral techniques when appropriate, to reduce tension and improve jaw comfort and mobility."
---

Treatment for TMJ dysfunction addresses the muscles of mastication and surrounding structures, including the masseter, temporalis, and cervical musculature, to reduce hypertonicity and improve jaw mobility and comfort. Intraoral techniques may be used to access the medial and lateral pterygoids, which are difficult to reach externally. These techniques are performed with gloves and informed consent. Home care strategies are often included, and referral to a dentist or other practitioner is made when indicated.
