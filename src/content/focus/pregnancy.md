Prenatal massage can ease low back and hip discomfort, leg fatigue, and general muscular tension while supporting relaxation during a time of significant physical change. Treatment uses side-lying or face down positioning and supportive bolstering, with modifications appropriate to each trimester. Care is coordinated with your prenatal care provider as needed.
