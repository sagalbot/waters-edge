Massage can support recovery from sprains and strains by easing protective muscle spasm, reducing pain, and helping you move with more comfort and confidence as you heal. As healing progresses, treatment can help restore range of motion and prepare you to return to activity alongside a graded rehabilitation plan. Technique, depth, and focus are adjusted to the stage of the injury, and referral is made when imaging or further assessment is warranted.
