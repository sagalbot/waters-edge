Massage therapy can help down-regulate the sympathetic nervous system and promote parasympathetic activity, which is associated with slower breathing, a lower heart rate, and reduced muscle guarding. Many people also report improved mood, a greater sense of calm, and better sleep. Massage works well alongside other supports such as counselling, breathwork, and regular movement.
