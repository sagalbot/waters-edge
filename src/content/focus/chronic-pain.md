Massage therapy for chronic pain works largely through the nervous system. Skilled touch can influence how the brain and spinal cord process pain signals, reduce protective muscle guarding, and promote a sense of safety and relaxation. This may lower pain sensitivity and make movement feel easier and more comfortable. Massage is most effective as part of a broader plan that includes graded movement, self-care strategies, and collaboration with other healthcare providers. Treatment is tailored to your pain presentation, tolerance, and goals.
